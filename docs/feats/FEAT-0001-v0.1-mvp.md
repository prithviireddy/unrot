# FEAT-0001: v0.1 MVP, blocking Reels and Shorts on Android
**Status:** In Progress. The code is built, the unit tests pass (216 tests), and the bar redraw, Simple patch, saved bar pictures and the fast block are in. Tested on the owner's Realme RMX3370 (Android 13) and OnePlus CPH2573 (Android 16) during development; the rows still marked ☐ in the acceptance table are the device checks left to do.

## Outcome
Someone who uses Instagram and YouTube for friends and long videos stops getting pulled into Reels and Shorts. The Reels and Shorts tabs look gone, short-form content closes wherever it opens, the Instagram feed stops when they're caught up, and a reel a friend sends still plays.

## Scope
1. Separate on/off for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and "stop at all caught up".
2. **Bottom bar redrawn without Reels or Shorts** (Instagram and YouTube). The bar's tab pictures are cropped from screenshots and saved on the phone. Where the redraw can't run (Android 8–10, Simple patch, or a bar that can't be redrawn safely), a **patch** in the bar colour covers the tab instead, and swallows taps. See [0002](../decisions/0002-cover-tab-icon-plus-hard-block.md), [0008](../decisions/0008-redraw-bottom-bar-sampled-colour.md) and [0009](../decisions/0009-save-bar-pictures-on-phone.md).
3. **Hard block** (Back plus a brief message, escalating to Home) for:
   - the Reels tab and the reel viewer opened from the feed, Explore or a profile
   - the Shorts tab and the Shorts player
   - a fast block from a target event, decided once two block probes agree (see [0010](../decisions/0010-provisional-bar-swallows-taps.md) for the bar's taps)
4. **The DM exception:** one reel from a DM plays, and swiping is blocked. See [0003](../decisions/0003-dm-one-reel-exception.md).
5. **Stop at caught up:** taps Home to jump the feed back to the top. See [0007](../decisions/0007-stop-at-all-caught-up.md).
6. **Friction:** a 30-second countdown on any weakening change. See [0004](../decisions/0004-friction-before-weakening.md).
7. A "blocked today" counter.
8. **Onboarding:**
   - the prominent disclosure
   - the accessibility setup, including Android 13+ restricted settings
   - battery settings, with guides for Xiaomi, Samsung and Oppo/Realme/OnePlus
   - the notification permission
9. **A watchdog notification** if the service stops while blocking is switched on.
10. **Rules as bundled JSON** with per-version rule sets and the remote-update plumbing. `RULES_URL` stays empty for now. See [0006](../decisions/0006-no-backend-remote-json-rules.md).
11. **The hidden Inspector:** tap the version text 7 times, then the DUMP button saves the screen as JSON, which can be shared from the app.

## Non-goals
- The Shorts shelf in the YouTube home feed.
- Schedules, daily allowances, "time saved" stats and a soft-nudge mode.
- TikTok, Facebook Reels and Snapchat Spotlight.
- A web wrapper, iOS or desktop.
- Hosting the rules remotely, the Play Store listing and the friend test. These are separate FEATs.

All of these are in [`../later.md`](../later.md) or the FEAT candidates.

## Risks
- **Rules on device:** the bundled rules come from dumps; the device checks in the table may still need rule fixes ([fix-bug skill](../../.claude/skills/fix-bug/SKILL.md), [capture dumps runbook](../runbooks/capture-inspector-dumps.md)).
- **Bar redraw ([0008](../decisions/0008-redraw-bottom-bar-sampled-colour.md)):** on Android 11–13 the overlay hides for about one frame to re-capture tabs; tap forwarding breaks if Instagram or YouTube redesign the bar. The `mode: patch` rule is the fallback. Remote rules are not live yet, so that fallback ships in the APK.
- **DM swipe detection:** other scroll events in the viewer might count as a swipe.
- **Accepted residual ([plan 0012](../plans/0012-final-review-fixes.md)):** a change inside the same window that sends no window-state event is not caught by the action check (see A16–A20 for the device checks).
